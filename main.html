Vikram is a Tamil action thriller movie directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj. The story follows a black-ops team investigating a series of murders connected to a dangerous drug syndicate.

Actors

Kamal Haasan plays the lead role as Vikram. Vijay Sethupathi plays Santhanam, and Fahadh Faasil plays Amar.
